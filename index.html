04

Turn physical experience into context.

Harness infrastructure + context scaffold

Steering reveals a capacity to adapt, but long tasks still involve repeated retries. The harness separates acting from diagnosing physical outcomes, then maintains conditional lessons that later actions can reuse.

In the displayed bread rollout, the first confirmed placement takes 144 calls. This motivates the harness; it is not a controlled measurement of its effect.

07

Better perception does not yet resolve fine contact.

Precision manipulation remains an open boundary.

The agent changes viewpoint, tests engagement, diagnoses slip and reindexes the held key. Yet visual alignment and successful arm motion do not establish stable seating or screw tightening.

Evidence, definitions & source records

69 recorded episodes in the October archive. September towel and barbecue cases, the supplied spatial cases, and bread/chip recordings are separate evidence and are not pooled. Decision paths summarize recorded action notes, not a reconstruction of hidden reasoning.

Demonstration replays use ordered input frames; full human demonstration movies are absent. Sponge trails track a visible edge in image space, not contact force or calibrated world coordinates. Gaps indicate occlusion. Input and robot views are not pixel-aligned.

Bread includes prior memories and two operator corrections. The available data do not contain matched baseline, ASPIRE or ENPIRE bread trials. Between-method first-success time, token cost and statistical significance remain to be evaluated.
